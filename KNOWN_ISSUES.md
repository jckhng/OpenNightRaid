# Known Issues

This is a playable initial release, not a claim of complete equivalence to
the DOS game. Comparisons cover recorded routes and selected scenes, not every
input sequence, level, platform, or original data revision.

## Intermission Timing

Smart-bomb destruction pictures match the original when aligned by animation
phase, but the delay before destruction and the first rubble-image hold still
differ in the tested scene. Three-trooper overrun visible-hold timing also
remains open. Matching pictures does not establish matching timing.

## Menus And Audio

A recorded option-cancel comparison leaves the control-panel selection on the
first row instead of the original's second row. The stored music-toggle value
also differs in that comparison; its meaning and timing remain unresolved.
The complete Escape, menu, and audio-toggle matrix has not been verified.

Audio passes the current correlation and content checks, but sample-identical
output has not been established. These checks do not certify every sound path
or packaged build.

## Coverage And Original Data

Broader natural gameplay, random-number lifecycle, input sequences, and uncommon
intermission variants remain incompletely tested. Controlled scene comparisons
are not full-campaign certification or uninterrupted original timing captures.

Only the registered and shareware archive revisions listed in the
[README](README.md#requirements) are verified. Shareware supports its original
four levels and dedicated ending; it does not provide registered-only levels.

## Deliberate Enhanced Differences

Faithful mode retains the original missed-fire behavior. Enhanced mode buffers
short fire presses and adds presentation effects and other quality-of-life
changes. Switching presentation with F12 does not disable enhanced input fixes;
use the faithful executable for the closest recovered original behavior.

See [comparison notes](docs/COMPARISON_NOTES.md) for the scope and presentation
of the published videos, and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for
licenses. Original game material is not covered by the project-source MIT grant.
