# What The Comparisons Show

These media illustrate specific recorded comparisons, not a full-game fidelity
percentage. See [known issues](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md) for remaining limitations.

## Missed Cannon Fire

The original DOS game misses four short Space taps between firing gates.
Faithful mode reproduces those misses; enhanced mode buffers the taps until
the next gate without increasing the firing rate. Holding Space works in all
three. The comparison uses the original's observed processed controls, not a
measurement of physical keyboard latency across platforms.

The clip is silent and shown at 8x slow motion. Missing intermediate original
pictures are held until the next captured page. Shot counters come from actor
traces, not visual guesses. All 134 completed original pages match the faithful
render before video compression; the GIF is a reduced preview.

## Three-Way Gameplay

The gameplay clip shows one 20-second level-one route at normal speed.
Both native simulations match across its 1,400 updates, and all 1,400 faithful
source pictures match the selected original pages before video compression.

The video uses one faithful-build soundtrack with no pitch or speed changes;
it does not compare or mix the three soundtracks. Original page holds follow
the nominal 70 Hz reconstruction, not the debugger's wall time. Enhanced effects
are captured at 70 Hz, so live 120 Hz interpolation is not represented.

## My Pa's Scores

The screenshot shows the original DOS high-score screen using my pa's save,
with six `LY` entries and a best score of 4,855. The save remains private and
unchanged; enlargement preserves the pixel edges.

## Media Rights

Selected screenshots and videos contain original Night Raid artwork and audio.
They are documentation media, not an extracted asset pack, and are excluded
from the project-source MIT grant. Original archives, executables, raw saves,
decoded resources, and debugging dumps are not distributed.
